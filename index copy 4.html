A STORY UNFOLDING

The severe mercy of God

I never planned to write about pain. But sometimes the stories we need to tell are the ones we never expected to live.

Not the mercy that soothes but the mercy that closes doors we would have walked through forever.

This is a place for writing shaped under pressure. For witness over performance. For those navigating loss, rebuilding, or the quiet work of becoming aligned again.
