    A Severe Act of Mercy

    Currently being written

    This memoir tells the story of how God's mercy shaped my life through unexpected loss and faith. It explores the tension between what we plan and what God calls us to, and the strange gifts that emerge when we learn to surrender.

    • You struggle with faith in hard seasons
    • You're learning to surrender control
    • You seek spiritual transformation

    Not For You If

    • You seek entertainment only

    • You avoid difficult truths

    ABOUT

    A Story Still Being Written

    John Richard King is an author and speaker exploring the intersection of faith, loss, and transformation. His work focuses on how God's mercy shapes us through unexpected seasons of difficulty and change.

    For the past fifteen years, John has been involved in spiritual formation work, helping others navigate the difficult terrain between what they planned and what God is calling them to. He believes that our stories matter, and that the hard chapters are often the most sacred.

    When not writing or speaking, John spends time with his family, reading theology and literature, and walking in nature. He is currently working on his memoir and weekly devotional reflections.
