    About the Book

    A Severe Act of Mercy is a memoir that explores the intersection of faith, loss, and transformation. Through a series of interconnected stories, John Richard King invites readers into his journey of learning to surrender control and trust in God's mercy, even when that mercy comes through unexpected seasons of difficulty.

    The book opens with a moment of profound loss and traces how that loss became a doorway into deeper faith. It's a story about what happens when our plans collide with God's calling, and how the grace we receive in those moments shapes us in ways we could never have anticipated.

    Why This Book?

    In a culture that celebrates control and self-determination, this memoir offers a different perspective. It's for anyone who has experienced loss and struggled to find meaning in it. It's for those who feel called to something larger than themselves but are unsure what that calling means. And it's for readers who believe that our stories—especially the hard chapters—matter deeply.

    King's writing combines spiritual reflection with honest vulnerability. He doesn't offer easy answers or quick fixes. Instead, he invites readers to sit with difficult questions and discover that God's mercy often looks different than we expect.
