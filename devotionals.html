    • Devotionals

    Weekly spiritual reflections on faith, formation, and God's presence in our lives.

    Weekly Reflections

    Join us for weekly devotional reflections exploring themes of faith, loss, mercy, and spiritual formation. Available in two formats: brief written reflections sent via email, and recorded audio devotionals for listening.

    Each week's reflection focuses on a single theme or question, inviting you into deeper spiritual contemplation and practice. These are not quick fixes or surface-level inspirational quotes, but rather thoughtful meditations designed to shape how we understand God's work in our lives.

    Voice Devotionals

    Listen to John Richard King's voice as he shares weekly reflections on faith and formation. These 5-10 minute recordings are available on your favorite podcast platform, offering a personal connection to the author's thoughts and inviting you into a contemplative space.

    Perfect for morning reflection, commutes, or evening prayer, voice devotionals provide a way to engage with spiritual themes in the midst of your daily life.

    Written Devotionals

    Receive weekly written reflections in your email inbox. These more detailed essays allow for deeper exploration of themes and offer space for personal journaling and reflection.

    Each written devotional includes the author's meditation, relevant Scripture passages, reflection questions, and a closing prayer. They're designed to be read slowly, savored, and lived with throughout the week.
